<p><strong>NOX</strong> is an Android VPN client with a Persian/English interface and support for Xray, OpenVPN, OpenConnect and WireGuard.</p>

<h3>Features</h3>
<ul>
<li>Import configurations from links, files, clipboard and QR codes</li>
<li>Manage subscriptions and refresh them automatically</li>
<li>Test server delays and select configurations using real test results</li>
<li>View connection duration, traffic counters and supported subscription usage</li>
<li>Persian right-to-left and English interface</li>
</ul>

<h3>Usage</h3>
<p>NOX does not provide VPN servers. Add your own subscription or configuration before connecting.</p>
